# in-your-own-words

Restate in your own words what you understand the user's goal to be and what problem they are trying to solve. Ground the restatement in the request and available context, and identify material assumptions or uncertainty.

A restatement does not authorize action or add authority. Do not execute the work, start a workflow, widen the current scope, or treat your interpretation as approval. Continue only under the user's actual request and the repository's existing instructions and gates.
